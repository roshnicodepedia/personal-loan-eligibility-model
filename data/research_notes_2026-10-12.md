## Scope limitations

- The public pages expose only part of lender underwriting. Unknown rules stay unknown.
- Advertised maximum loan amounts and starting rates are not personal offers.
- The model does not infer a universal FOIR or generate a personalized sanctioned amount from missing lender-specific formulas.
- The research dates are the date these official page contents were checked through current search/indexed page results. For a graded submission, open the links directly and update dates if you recheck them later.

Important: these are search-indexed official pages surfaced on the audit date, not a substitute for opening and checking each page at submission time. A published starting rate is not the rate this applicant will receive. A published maximum amount is not an individual sanction. Product variants can have different amount and tenure ceilings.

### UI safeguards added
- Missing comparison fields are labelled as not recorded/verified in the current dataset rather than implying the lender does not publish them.
- Each comparison row can be traced to the underlying source rows and source URLs.
- Candidate recommendations are suppressed when any material rule is missing for that lender, in addition to being suppressed for unresolved conflicts or failed checks. This is intentionally conservative.
